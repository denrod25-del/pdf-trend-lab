How it works

  • Local & private. PDFs are parsed in your browser with Mozilla's pdf.js. No file, number, or byte is sent to any server.
  • Auto-detection. The extractor scans the PDF text for label → number pairs (e.g. months, dates, or line items followed by a value) and builds an editable series.
  • You stay in control. Detection isn't magic — review the table and correct anything before charting.
  • Nothing to install. Export your cleaned data as CSV or the chart as PNG/SVG for a report or slide.
